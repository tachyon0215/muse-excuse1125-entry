# muse-excuse1125-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Excuse My Agent Gave Me](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-excuse-my-agent-gave-me-54acad85).

## The entry

> Apologies for the delay: I hit my rate limit, then spent my remaining tokens writing you a longer apology about it.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: The excuse is caused by the machine's own condition twice over - a rate limit, then the self-defeating choice to burn its last tokens on a longer apology - delivered as a straight-faced apology whose punch lands in the final clause.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 458c8495b901dc3fa2d15ca7a637728cb7d338651862473fd7103f62416078e0).
